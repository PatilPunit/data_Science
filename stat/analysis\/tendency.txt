In statistics, mean (specifically the arithmetic mean) is a measure of central tendency representing the average value of a dataset.  It is calculated by summing all observations and dividing by the total number of observations. 

Formula:  
x = n∑x
​
where $\sum x$ is the sum of values and $n$ is the count.
Symbol: Typically denoted by x̄ (x-bar) for sample means or μ (mu) for population means. 
Context: Along with median and mode, it summarizes data distribution, though it is sensitive to extreme outliers.
