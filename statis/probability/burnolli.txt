1. Bernoulli Distribution
Definition

A Bernoulli distribution models an experiment that has only two possible outcomes:

Success (1)
Failure (0)

It is the simplest probability distribution and serves as the foundation for the Binomial distribution.

Conditions
Only one trial is performed.
There are exactly two outcomes.
Probability of success remains constant.
Parameters
p=P(Success)
q=1−p=P(Failure)
Probability Mass Function (PMF)
P(X=x)=p
x
(1−p)
1−x

where

x=0 or 1
Mean
E(X)=p
Variance
Var(X)=p(1−p)
Example

A coin is tossed once.

Let:

Head = Success
Tail = Failure

If the coin is fair:

p=0.5

Then the experiment follows a Bernoulli distribution.
