5. Exponential Distribution
Definition

The Exponential distribution models the waiting time until the next event occurs.

It is closely related to the Poisson distribution.

Relationship with Poisson

Poisson answers:

How many events occur?

Exponential answers:

How long until the next event occurs?

Memoryless Property

The Exponential distribution possesses the memoryless property:

P(X>s+t∣X>s)=P(X>t)

This means the future waiting time does not depend on how long you have already waited.

Example

A customer service center receives on average:

10 calls/hour

The Exponential distribution can be used to find the probability that the next call arrives within 5 minutes.

Applications
Time between phone calls
Time between machine failures
Waiting time for buses
