2. Binomial Distribution
Definition

A Binomial distribution models the number of successes in a fixed number of independent Bernoulli trials.

It is essentially a collection of Bernoulli experiments.

Conditions
Fixed number of trials (n)
Two outcomes per trial
Trials are independent
Probability of success remains constant
Parameters
n=Number of trials
p=Probability of success
q=1−p

Relationship Between Bernoulli and Binomial

Bernoulli:

One trial

Binomial:

Multiple Bernoulli trials

Example:

Tossing a coin once → Bernoulli
Tossing a coin 100 times → Binomial
